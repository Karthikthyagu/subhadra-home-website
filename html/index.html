25+ Years of Caring Excellence

For over 25 years, J Bharathi has dedicated her life to caring for the elderly with compassion and dignity. At a time when many aging individuals are neglected or seen as a burden, Subhadra Home stands as a place of love, support, and respect.

Bharathi says, “From a young age, I always wanted to help others. I bought books for children and bus passes for the elderly. Nearly 30 years ago, a group of friends and I started a home in Neelankarai.”

Over the years, the friends who helped establish the home moved on in different directions, but Bharathi, a resident of Kottivakkam, continued her selfless service with unwavering dedication.
