WELCOME TO SUBHADRA HOME

About Subhadra Home

More than a care home — a family built on love and dignity.

Established 1997

25+ Years of Caring Excellence

J Bharathi (61), founder of Subhadra Home, is a shining light when families see aging souls as a burden. Many assume only financial worries push families to leave seniors, but this home proves otherwise.

Bharathi says, "Growing up, I was always interested in helping others. I bought books for children and bus passes for the elderly. Thirty years ago, a group of friends and I created a home in Neelankarai."

Over time, the seven friends who began the home eventually went their separate ways. But Bharathi, a resident of Kottivakkam, continued her dedicated service.
