OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive Care Services

At Subhadra Home, we offer a complete range of care services designed to ensure comfort, dignity, and safety for every resident. Our holistic approach addresses physical health, emotional well-being, and social engagement, creating an environment where your loved ones can thrive.

Assisted Living

Support with daily activities while maintaining independence. Our compassionate staff provides personalized assistance with bathing, dressing, medication reminders, and mobility support, ensuring residents live with dignity and comfort.

  • ✓24/7 professional caregiving support
  • ✓Personalized daily activity assistance
  • ✓Safe and accessible living spaces
  • ✓Independence with dignity and respect

24/7 Medical Supervision

Round-the-clock medical monitoring with regular doctor visits, medication management, and emergency care protocols. Our experienced healthcare team ensures comprehensive medical support at all times.

  • ✓Regular doctor visits and health assessments
  • ✓24/7 emergency care and rapid response
  • ✓Hospital tie-ups for specialized care
  • ✓Medication monitoring and management

Physiotherapy & Mobility Support

Exercise, mobility training, and rehabilitation sessions tailored to individual needs. Our physiotherapy support helps residents maintain strength, flexibility, and independence through personalized programs.

  • ✓One-on-one physiotherapy sessions
  • ✓Customized mobility training
  • ✓Fall prevention and balance exercises
  • ✓Pain management therapy

Nutritious & Personalized Meals

Diet plans customized to health requirements, prepared fresh daily by our kitchen staff. We cater to dietary restrictions, preferences, and medical needs while ensuring every meal is delicious and nutritious.

  • ✓Customized meal plans
  • ✓Fresh home-cooked meals
  • ✓Dietary accommodations
  • ✓Nutritionist consultations

Mental Wellness & Emotional Support

Counselling, group activities, and companionship programs designed to promote cognitive health and emotional well-being. We create a supportive environment that nurtures mental sharpness and positive spirits.

  • ✓Individual and group counselling
  • ✓Cognitive stimulation activities
  • ✓Memory care programs
  • ✓Emotional support

Recreational Activities

Yoga, games, arts, music, social events, and celebrations that bring joy and meaning to daily life. Our activity calendar helps residents stay engaged, active, and socially connected.

  • ✓Daily yoga classes
  • ✓Arts and creative workshops
  • ✓Music and cultural programs
  • ✓Festival celebrations

Family Engagement Programs

Regular family visits, updates, and bonding activities to maintain strong family connections. We believe family involvement is crucial to resident happiness and provide multiple ways for families to stay connected.

  • ✓Flexible visiting hours
  • ✓Regular family updates
  • ✓Family counselling
  • ✓Special family events

End-of-Life / Hospice Care

Compassionate support ensuring dignity, comfort, and peace during the final stages of life. Our care approach focuses on respect, empathy, and emotional support for residents and their families.

  • ✓Compassionate palliative care
  • ✓Pain management support
  • ✓Emotional and spiritual support
  • ✓Family guidance
