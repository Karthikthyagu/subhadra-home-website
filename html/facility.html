SAFETY FIRST

Built for Safety & Accessibility

Every corner of Subhadra Home is designed with senior safety and accessibility as the top priority.

Accessibility

All areas feature ramps, handrails, non-slip flooring, and wide corridors for wheelchair access. Emergency call buttons are installed in every room.

Hygiene Standards

We follow strict sanitation protocols with daily cleaning, eco-friendly products, and maintain high standards of cleanliness throughout.

Personalization

Residents can bring personal belongings and photos to make their room feel like home. We respect each resident's preferences and privacy.
